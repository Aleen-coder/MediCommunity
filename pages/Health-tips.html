Important Health Tips

Please remember: This information is for general guidance only and does not constitute medical advice. Always follow the specific instructions provided by your doctor or healthcare team.

Before Surgery

  • Avoid Heavy Meals: Don’t eat large meals before surgery. ⚠️
  • Inform Your Doctor: Share all medications, supplements, and herbal remedies.

After Surgery

  • Hydration: Drink small sips of water frequently unless advised otherwise. 💧
  • Gentle Walking: Light movement helps circulation. 🚶‍♀️
  • Follow Diet Plan: Stick to your doctor’s post-op dietary instructions.
